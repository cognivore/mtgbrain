# oddysey2026 — Post-2004 Creature Filler Proposal (508 → 540)

A deliberate-exception list: **35 post-2004 mono-colour creatures** (7 each of W/U/B/R/G) proposed as
low-power, on-theme filler to take the cube from **508 → ~540**. The cube normally bars post-2004
creatures; every card here earns its slot by serving a specific dream-archetype role at the cube's
very-low power line — not by being good.

**Count math:** 35 candidates overshoots 540 by 3 (→ 543). Cut your 3 least-favourite to land exactly
on **540**, or keep all 35 on the way to **585** (45 × 13). All 35 are verified in the DB: mono-colour
(`color_count=1`), `Creature`, first printing 2005+, and **absent from the live cube** (`cube_editor.sqlite`,
`removed=0`).

**Archetype reading used (per your corrections):**
- **UR = BIG RED**, not "spells matter": rituals/mana, mana-sink bodies, self-mill/rummage toward a
  Laboratory-Maniac/Jace empty-library win, moderate Grapeshot backup.
- **WB = LIFE LOSS / Death's Shadow**: paying/losing life as a resource — **not** lifegain.

### Honest gaps to weigh
- **WB Life Loss is thin (1).** Black leaned hard into hellbent (its Torment identity), so the
  pay-life/lose-life theme rests on Adanto Vanguard. If you want it deeper, swap a redundant hellbent
  black (e.g. Slaughterhouse Bouncer or Necromancer's Familiar) for a life-loss black body — say the
  word and I'll source mono-B "you lose life" / Death's-Shadow-enabling creatures.
- **WR Aggro is thin (1)** by design — the cube isn't trying to be a creature-aggro deck.
- **BR is overweight (9).** Easiest place to cut the 3 needed to hit exactly 540.
