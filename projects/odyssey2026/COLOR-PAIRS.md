# oddysey2026 — The Ten Colour Pairs

Each guild has a distinct identity, an engine, and an **honest win condition**. Decks are *not on
rails* — these are the spines, but the connective cards (counters, discard, coin-flips, tokens,
fixing) let pairs bleed into each other. Card names below are all in the 360.

---

## WU — Azorius · "Enchantress Replenish Prison"
**Plan:** assemble a soft-lock out of enchantments, then bury them under a one-shot recursion.
- **Engine:** Mesa Enchantress / Sigil draw, Sterling Grove + Enlightened Tutor toolbox, Auramancer.
- **The lock:** **Opalescence** (→UW) + **Humility** = every creature is a 1/1 with no abilities;
  **Opalescence + Enchanted Evening** (errata: ETB mill 3 + return an enchantment) = symmetric
  Armageddon. **Sphere of Safety / Ghostly Prison / Teferi's Moat / Dovescape / Solitary Confinement**
  keep you alive while it comes together.
- **Payoff / win:** **Replenish** (mass-return all your enchantments in one shot), **Sigil of the
  Empty Throne**, **Starfield of Nyx**; or the alt-wins **Test of Endurance** / **Azor's Elocutors**.
- **Plays like:** the patient prison deck — durdle, tax, then combo-lock and grind out.

## UB — Dimir · "Honest Control-Combo / Deck-Out"
**Plan:** draw-go control that durdles to a fragile, telegraphed combo turn.
- **Engine:** Cephalid self-mill (**Cephalid Looter/Vandal/Sage**), **Standstill**, **Day's Undoing**,
  card-tutors (**Tainted Pact, Demonic Consultation**), **Narcomoeba** off the mill.
- **The win:** **Ad Nauseam** → **Laboratory Maniac / Jace, Wielder of Mysteries** (after **Mana
  Severance** empties your lands) or **Lightning Storm**; **High Tide → Brain Freeze**; **Doomsday**;
  **Leveler + Sundial of the Infinite**; **Sphinx's Tutelage / Glimpse the Unthinkable** mill.
- **Tax:** **Teferi's Puzzle Box + Phyrexian Tyranny** (→U/B) punish the forced redraw.
- **Plays like:** blue-black durdle that suddenly wins from an empty board.

## BR — Rakdos · "Hellbent Graveyard-Storm + Bridge Prison"
**Plan:** empty your hand on purpose — it powers both a storm kill *and* a prison.
- **Engine:** discard outlets (**Putrid Imp, Patrol Hound, Minotaur Explorer, Faithless Looting,
  Burning Inquiry**), rituals (**Dark / Cabal / Songs of the Damned / Pyretic / Seething Song**),
  **One With Nothing + Shadow of the Grave** (errata loop), **Infernal Tutor + Lion's Eye Diamond**.
- **Win A (storm):** **Tendrils of Agony / Grapeshot / Empty the Warrens**.
- **Win B (prison):** **Ensnaring Bridge** (→B/R) with an empty hand shuts off their attackers, while
  **Black Vise / The Rack / Ebony Owl Netsuke** (→B), **Cursed Scroll**, and Lantern mill close it.
- **Plays like:** a glass-cannon storm deck *or* a hellbent lock — often both in one 40.

## BG — Golgari · "Smokestack Sacrifice + Attrition"
**Plan:** turn your own cheap permanents into a resource advantage the opponent can't match.
- **Engine:** fodder (**Cabal Trainee, Crypt Creeper, Acorn Harvest tokens**) + outlets (**Braids,
  Cabal Minion; Cabal Archon; Cabal Patriarch**), recursion (**Gravedigger, Cabal Surgeon**).
- **The lock:** **Smokestack** (→B/G) grinds the board to zero — and you're built to lose less.
  **Pernicious Deed** resets, **Squandered Resources** bursts, counter-movers (**Forgotten Ancient,
  Quarry Hauler**) shuffle −1/−1s and proliferate.
- **Plays like:** aristocrats-stax; inevitability through symmetric sacrifice you're ahead on.

## RG — Gruul · "Big-Red Mana Sink"
**Plan:** make escalating mana every turn and pour it into sinks until something overflows.
- **Engine:** **Braids of Fire** (cumulative upkeep, +1 red each turn), storage lands, **Mage-Ring
  Network, Worn Powerstone**; ramp (**Werebear, Wild Growth, Rampant Growth, Utopia Sprawl**).
- **The sinks/payoffs:** **Comet Storm, Kamahl Pit Fighter, Chainflinger**; cumulative-upkeep age
  bombs **Magmatic Core / Heart of Bogardan**; **Stormbind / Aether Rift**; threshold/madness fatties.
- **Plays like:** ramp that doesn't cast one big thing but *drips* mana into a dozen small overloads.

## GW — Selesnya · "Opposition Tokens"
**Plan:** flood the board with little bodies, then tap the opponent out of the game.
- **Engine:** token-makers (**Squirrel Nest, Nut Collector, Squirrel Wrangler, Druid's Call**) and
  flashback tokens (**Call of the Herd, Chatter of the Squirrel, Acorn Harvest, Elephant Ambush,
  Grizzly Fate, Roar of the Wurm**).
- **The payoff:** **Opposition** (→G/W) — tap their lands and blockers every turn; **Glory of
  Warfare / Pianna / Squirrel Mob** anthems push the win; **Sterling Grove / Glittering Wish** toolbox.
- **Plays like:** go-wide that converts a board into a tap-down prison, then swarms.

## WB — Orzhov · "Life as a Resource"
**Plan:** treat life as mana — spend it, drain theirs, then swap totals.
- **Engine:** pay-life card draw (**Greed, Dark Tutelage, Bond of Agony, Font of Agonies**), Clerics
  to stay alive, **Final Payment / Death Grasp** interaction.
- **The win:** **Mirror Universe / Soul Conduit** — drop low, then swap; **Death's Shadow** is huge at
  low life; **Laquatus's Champion** drains; **Rain of Gore / Tainted Remedy** punish their lifegain;
  alt-wins **Test of Endurance / Near-Death Experience / Transcendence**.
- **Plays like:** a knife's-edge attrition deck that wins *because* it's nearly dead.

## UR — Izzet · "Spells & Coin-Flips"
**Plan:** chain cheap/free spells for tempo, storm, or a luck-fueled blowout.
- **Engine:** free spells (**Snap, Frantic Search, Capsize, Reset**), **Merchant Scroll / Mystic
  Speculation**, **High Tide** overlap, **Scrivener** rebuy.
- **The win:** **Brain Freeze / Grapeshot / Empty the Warrens** storm; **Lightning Storm** Ad-Nauseam
  finish; coin-flips **Stitch in Time** (extra turn), **Fiery Gambit, Game of Chaos** + **Krark's
  Thumb**; **Epic Experiment**; **Fevered Visions** wheel-burn.
- **Plays like:** tempo-combo that can win fair or off a wild flip.

## WR — Boros · "Tax Aggro"
**Plan:** a clock plus tax pieces that price the opponent out of interacting or attacking back.
- **Engine:** aggressive weenies (Birds, Soldiers, dwarves, threshold beaters) behind **Cage of Hands,
  Ghostly Prison, Sphere of Safety, Suppression Field, Sphere of Resistance**.
- **The grind:** **Goblin Trenches** (sac-land tokens) + **Glory of Warfare**; **Cage of Hands**
  tax-attack lockdown (#15) keeps their best blocker tapped down.
- **Plays like:** prison-aggro — the fastest clock in the cube, still measured by its standards.

## UG — Simic · "Proliferate & Fading Counters"
**Plan:** put counters on everything and proliferate them toward a win — or a soft-lock.
- **Engine:** **Steady Progress, Tezzeret's Gambit, Contagion Clasp/Engine, Throne of Geth, Karn's
  Bastion, Nesting Grounds**; counter-movers **Forgotten Ancient, Quarry Hauler, Chlorophant**.
- **The payoffs:** **Simic Ascendancy** (20 growth = win), **Darksteel Reactor** (20 charge),
  **Helix Pinnacle** (100 tower); **Tangle Wire** (→U/G) — fade counters you re-arm with proliferate
  for a soft mana-lock; **Saproling Burst / Parallax** fading bridges.
- **Plays like:** a counters engine that ticks toward an alt-win while Tangle Wire holds them back.

---

### Cross-pair connective tissue (why decks aren't on rails)
- **Proliferate** (UG) supercharges every counter win *and* fade counters *and* storage lands.
- **Discard outlets** feed graveyard-storm, madness, hellbent, *and* Ensnaring Bridge.
- **Coin-flips** feed Chance Encounter, Goblin Bomb, Krark's Thumb, Stitch in Time.
- **Tokens** feed Opposition, Smokestack fodder, Goblin Trenches, and go-wide anthems.
- **Life payment** feeds Ad Nauseam, Death's Shadow, Bond of Agony, and the Mirror Universe swap.
