Hey, I figured that since I am buidling a cube with alternate wincons which can break any stalls and weak odyssey / onslaught era creatures; I actually can experiment with graveyard storm theme (make One With Nothing playable if not actively good) and I can go nuts with Smokestack and Tanglewire (I think your previous analysis of "fun counters" completely missed Fading counters?!). I can also experiment with Braids of Fire (cumulative upkeep counters / age counters! How everything plays well into itself) for some sort of big red / mana sink deck, maybe trading post, maybe something like this? I can also experiment with Opposition, Ensnaring Bridge, etc. I would like for Opposition, ensnaring bridge.

I also will probably be adding colours to Ensnaring Bridge, Tanglewire and Smokestacks.

So here is a specout for cube for you —

 1. Braids of Fire playable and strong option (can proliferate a bunch of counters onto it in late game, enchantment matters)
 2. One With Nothing is playable and strong option for graveyard storm (you can check out my sketches for graveyard storm in my norin cube https://www.cubecobra.com/cube/list/wary and probably improve on it?
 3. High Tide is playable and strong option (maybe some sort of prisony stuff which turns lands into Islands as an archetype and High Tide as one of payoffs?)
 4. Ad Nauseam both with Tendrils and in the Modern Deck version where the win happens through Lightning Storm or through Laboratory Maniac (never Thassa's Oracle) is strong and playable in this cube
 5. Opposition is colour-shifted to whichever creaturebase from this cube "shits out" the most creatures by turn four https://cubecobra.com/cube/about/odysseyblock?view=primer [Opposition is now a gold card].
 6. Tanglewire is colour-shifted to the colours which proliferate the most in your plan [Tanglewire is now a gold artifact].
 7. Smokestack is colour-shifted to the colours which remove counter or move counters between the permanents [Smokestack is now a gold artifact]
 8. Ensnaring bridge is colour-shifted to the colours which want to discard their hand [Ensnaring bridge is now a gold artifact]
 9. Unless absolutely necessary, there are no creatures outside odyssey block. For example, currently Narcomeba is necessary because it is a small payoff for self-mill; 
 10. Enchanted Evening Opalesence Humility Replenish bullshit is going on in UW deck; Opalescence is colourshifted to UW; Enchanted Evening is errattad to also put top 3 cards of the deck into the yard and then bring an enchantment into player's hand.
 11. Mirror Universe is a playable and strong option in a life-loss deck which is real enough to also make Death's Shadow a viable option (as well as Shadow of the Industry).
 12. Colour pairs do diverse things, decks are not on rails, cards synergyse with each other's weirdness, if some card does literally nothing (like alternate wincons), mark it for erratta and I will give adjustments so that each card does something when it enters, is a mana sink (for example for big red deck) or something like this.
 13. Sundial of the Infinite is playable and does things. For example, it can be another Stifle for Leveler, which is an alternative for Ad Nauseam / Spoils / Angel's Grace / Labman nonsense.
 14. Teferi's puzzlebox lock is achievable (find the colour home for artifacts there and also colour shift artifacts to 1 or more colours).
 15. Lantern / Fateseal behind Ensnaring Bridge (and maybe like tax attacks like Cage of Hands?) is a way to genuninely win. [Probably some lantern cards will need erratta so that they do something on their own, following principle #16].
 16. Important principle — keep commitments like Ad Nauseam stapled on a spell; for example, Bolas's Citadel is a NO since it stays on the board over many turns; Thousand Year Storm is also no because it's like storming off with training wheels.
 17. Keep it singleton.
