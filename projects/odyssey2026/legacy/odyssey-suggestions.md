# Odyssey cube — saved card suggestions

Design target: **very low power** (Odyssey-Block band — commons/uncommons, slow build-arounds,
no fast mana/staples) but **fun and decision-intensive**. The cube is a counter / coin-flip /
proliferate / alt-win jank cube built around **Chance Encounter**.

Power tags below: `[band]` = fits the very-low power level · `[flex]` = stronger, include only if
you raise the band a bit · most are slow build-arounds that play fine even when individually punchy.

---

## 1. Gruul "Chance Encounter" package (red coin-flip alternate win)

Win coin flips → luck counters on Chance Encounter → multiply with proliferate / any-counter
doublers → win at 10. (Already in cube: **Chance Encounter**, **Powerful Broker**, **Quarry
Hauler**, **Dramatist's Puppet**.)

**Red win conditions (coin flip + counters)**
- Goblin Bomb — {1}{R} — *fuse* — upkeep flips add/remove fuse; 5 → 20 damage. `[band]`

**Win more flips → more counters**
- Krark's Thumb — {2} — flip two, ignore one (~75% wins); the key multiplier. `[band]`
- Goblin Traprunner — {3}{R} — three flips per attack. `[band]`
- Mirror March — {5}{R} — flip-until-you-lose on each creature ETB (wins = luck + token copies). `[band]`
- Okaun, Eye of Chaos — {4}{R} — flip-until-you-lose each combat (partner Zndrsplt is blue). `[band]`

## 3. From the `odysseyblock` reference cube (Odyssey Block, 450)

Vetted by a per-card panel against the low-power + decision-intensive bar.

**Add — funky non-+1/+1 scalers, decision-intensive, on-band `[band]`:**
- Bomb Squad — {3}{R} — *fuse* — tap to fuse a creature; fuses double each upkeep; at 4 → destroy
  it + 4 damage to its controller. A ticking time-bomb removal engine; your Broker/Quarry Hauler/
  Puppet can shove an opponent's creature to 4 for on-demand removal. (decisions 4/5)
- Cephalid Vandal — {1}{U} — *shred* — self-accelerating self-mill (1, 3, 6, 10…); feeds
  Mortal Combat / Narcomoeba / graveyard recursion. Remove shred counters to avoid decking — a
  real throttle. (decisions 4/5)
- Delaying Shield — {3}{W} — *delay* — banks incoming damage as counters, then each upkeep pay
  {1}{W} or lose 1 life per counter. A mana-vs-life puzzle that buys time for the slow alt-wins.
  (decisions 4/5)

**Add if you want the removable-shield +1/+1 sub-theme (interactive; proliferate/Broker re-arm them):**
- Phantom Nomad — {1}{W} — enters with two +1/+1; prevents ALL damage by removing one counter
  (renewable wall; sheds only one counter per source, so bait gang-blocks). (decisions 3/5)
- Phantom Flock — {3}{W}{W} — same shield mechanic on a 3/3 flyer. (decisions 3/5)

**Optional:**
- Savage Firecat — {3}{R}{R} — enters with seven +1/+1; tapping a land for mana removes one — a
  self-taxing diminishing clock (mana vs. board size). Funky use of +1/+1. (decisions 3/5)

**Skip:** Phantom Nishoba (too strong — 7 counters + lifegain + trample), Soulcatchers' Aerie
(no Bird density in the cube → scales nothing), Soulcatcher / Chlorophant / Nantuko Cultivator /
Repentant Vampire / Vampiric Dragon (plain +1/+1 growth, low decision; Vampiric Dragon also too
strong), and Circular Logic / Spelljack / Syncopate (counterspells, off-theme).
