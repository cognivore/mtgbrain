# {U}{B} Dimir — Discard Storm

A grindy Dimir deck that discards (its own cards and the opponent's), wheels and loots for value, and turns hand-disruption into life loss, mill, and storm-y payoff turns. We want discard *payoffs*, wheel/loot engines, hand-attack that feeds those payoffs, and control glue — never swingy bombs.

### Notes per pick

**Recoil** — Three mana to bounce *any* permanent and force the controller to discard a card is exactly the Dimir control-glue this deck wants: it answers a threat, strips a card, and can be aimed at the opponent's own returned bomb. No card advantage, pure tempo, perfectly fair for a low-power table.

**Seer's Vision** — Opponents play with hands revealed (huge information for a decision-dense cube), and you can sacrifice it to surgically strip their best card. The reveal-then-sac structure makes it a slow, telegraphed hand-attack engine rather than a bomb.

**Unfulfilled Desires** — A pre-modern repeatable loot engine: pay {1} and 1 life to draw-then-discard at instant speed. It's the cleanest "fills your own graveyard / triggers your discard payoffs" enabler in the historic UB pool, and the life cost keeps it honest.

**Doomsday Specter** — A 2/2 flyer that forces a targeted discard every time it connects, recurring hand-attack pressure. The ETB tax (return a U/B creature) and fragile body keep it low-power while rewarding evasive aggression that feeds the discard plan.

**Marsh Crocodile** — On entry it makes *each* player discard a card, a symmetric discard trigger that's easy to break with your own discard payoffs. Its other ETB (return a U/B creature) makes it a natural blink/bounce target to re-fire the discard; the 3/4 body is otherwise plain.

**Whispering Madness** — The signature wheel: every player discards their hand and redraws to the largest hand discarded, and cipher lets you recur it off an evasive creature. It's a discard-storm centerpiece (mass discard + refuel) while staying symmetric and fair. Flag: first printed in Gatecrash (2013), so newer than the old-frame target — include only if a 2013 card is acceptable.

**Breathstealer's Crypt** — Every drawn card is revealed, and revealed creatures get discarded unless the player pays 3 life. It's a symmetric soft-lock that taxes draws and punishes the opponent's hand — great control glue, low ceiling because it hits you too.

**Nebuchadnezzar** — Pay X, tap, name a card, and the opponent reveals X random cards and discards all matches. Repeatable targeted discard with a genuine guessing mini-game — extremely decision-dense and on-theme, and the mana cost plus randomness keep it weak.

**Lobotomy** — Names a card and exiles every copy from hand, graveyard, and library. It's premium hand-disruption that clears the way for a storm/control finish; slow and tempo-negative, so fair at low power, though it's the strongest "stax" effect here. Flag if you want only purely fair cards.

**Dimir Guildmage** — A two-mana hybrid body that turns excess mana into repeatable {3}{B} discard or {3}{U} draw. A clean, grindy engine for both halves of the archetype; the steep activation cost keeps it low-power. Flag: Ravnica (2005), just past the old-frame window.

**Consult the Necrosages** — Modal: target player draws two *or* discards two. Flexible enough to refill yourself or strip the opponent, and the most fairly costed dedicated discard-or-draw spell in the pool. Flag: Ravnica (2005).

**Urza's Guilt** — Both players draw two, discard three, then lose 4 life. A symmetric pseudo-wheel that triggers your discard payoffs and pings life totals — swingy and situational, but thematically a discard-storm engine you can break with payoffs like Faith of the Devoted / Bone Miser.

## Borderline / maybeboard

- **Diabolic Vision** ({U}{B}, Ice Age 1995) — top-five dig, not discard, but genuine old-frame UB card filtering / control glue.
- **Dimir Cutpurse** ({1}{U}{B}, Ravnica 2005) — combat damage = opponent discards, you draw; clean payoff body, but 2005-era.
- **Perplex** ({1}{U}{B}, Ravnica 2005) — counter unless they discard their *whole hand* + transmute; great disruption, just past window.
- **Psychic Drain** ({X}{U}{B}, Ravnica 2005) — scalable mill + lifegain deck-out finisher; mill not discard, 2005.
- **Glimpse the Unthinkable** ({U}{B}, Ravnica 2005) — mill 10; strong mill finisher but high-power for this cube and 2005.
- **Memory Sluice / Dream Salvage / River's Grasp** (Shadowmoor 2008) — solid mill/wheel-adjacent hybrids, but newer frame.
- **Stone Drake** (Portal Second Age 1998; DB lists only the MB2 reprint) — "discards a card for each spell you've cast this turn" is literally storm-flavored discard, but verify the old printing exists before using.
- **Brainbite** ({2}{U}{B}, Alara Reborn 2009) — reveal + discard + draw a card; perfectly on-theme, just newer.
