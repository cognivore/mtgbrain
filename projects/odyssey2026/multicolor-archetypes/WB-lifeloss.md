# {W}{B} Orzhov — Lifeloss / Life-as-a-Resource

Gold cards that pay life, drain life, and treat the life total as fuel — small symmetric punishers and lifeswing payoffs for a deck built around Mirror Universe, Ad Nauseam, The Last Ride, and Death's Shadow.

The true old-frame (pre-2004) WB gold pool is tiny (~10 cards total), so the best on-theme density lives in the **Ravnica Orzhov block (GPT 2006 / DIS 2006 / EVE 2008)**, which is post-cutoff but flagged below. All cards work as printed at very low power.

## Notes per pick

**Soul Link** — The cleanest old-frame lifegain enabler in the pair. Attach to any combatant and every point of damage it deals or takes becomes life. Feeds Mirror Universe / Ad Nauseam life buffers without doing anything broken on its own.

**Gerrard's Verdict** — Two-card discard that can gain up to 6 life if the opponent pitches lands. Pure decision density: a hand-attack spell that doubles as a lifeswing, exactly the Orzhov fuel idiom. Genuinely low-power for a one-mana-plus-WB sorcery.

**Putrid Warrior** — A 2/2 whose damage trigger lets you choose "each player loses 1 life" or "each player gains 1 life." A symmetric life-mover on a fragile body — turns combat into a knob you twist toward your alt-win plan. Very fair.

**Martyrs' Tomb** — The textbook "pay life as a resource" card: pay 2 life, prevent 1 damage to a creature. A repeatable life-burn outlet that turns your life total into a protective shield — synergizes directly with Death's Shadow / The Last Ride incentives to bleed yourself low.

**Selenia, Dark Angel** — Old-frame (Tempest) 3/3 flyer that pays 2 life to bounce itself; a recurring life-spend with evasion. Fragile enough for low power, and the self-bounce gives a repeatable way to drain your own total on purpose.

**Pillory of the Sleepless** (flag: Guildpact, 2006) — Pacifism that also says "at the beginning of your upkeep, you lose 1 life" — clean removal-plus-drain that ticks the enchanted creature's controller down each turn. Quintessentially Orzhov and gentle.

**Orzhov Guildmage** (flag: Guildpact, 2006) — A 2/2 with two activated abilities: pay {2}{W} to gain 1 life, or {2}{B} to drain everyone 1. Slow and mana-hungry — a perfect grindy, decision-dense engine rather than a bomb.

**Blind Hunter** (flag: Guildpact, 2006) — 2/2 flyer that drains 2 (gain 2) on entry, and again when its haunt target dies. Two gentle 2-point swings off one card; on-theme and low-impact.

**Agent of Masks** (flag: Guildpact, 2006) — Upkeep drain: each opponent loses 1, you gain that much. A slow, repeatable life engine on a 2/3 — exactly the kind of incremental life-as-resource payoff the deck wants.

**Souls of the Faultless** (flag: Guildpact, 2006) — 0/4 defender that converts incoming combat damage into life gained for you and life lost by the attacker. A punishing wall that swings the life totals in both directions without ever attacking.

**Batwing Brume** (flag: Eventide, 2008) — Hybrid fork: spend {W} to fog all combat damage, spend {B} to make each player lose 1 life per attacker, or both with {W}{B}. Highly skill-testing, mana-symbol-dependent, and a true life-swing trick. Strong fit despite the late date.

## Borderline / maybeboard

- **Purgatory** (MIR 1997, old-frame) — pay {4} and 2 life each upkeep to reanimate; the life-pay clause is on-theme but the card is a grindy graveyard engine more than a lifeloss payoff.
- **Circle of Despair** (MIR 1997, old-frame) — sac-a-creature damage prevention; tangential to the life theme but old-frame and very fair.
- **Castigate** (GPT 2006) — WB hand-attack, no life component; cut unless you want more disruption.
- **Vindicate** / **Mortify** / **Utter End** — premium removal, no life theme; out of archetype.
- **Ghost Council of Orzhova** ({W}{W}{B}{B}, GPT 2006) — 4/4 with a 1-point drain ETB and a sac-to-blink loop; borderline bomb-y for this cube, hence maybeboard.
