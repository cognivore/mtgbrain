# UR Izzet — Laboratory Storm

Gold two-colour finds for a spells-matter / draw-your-deck / storm-lite Izzet deck that wins on an
alt-win permanent or a slow spell-chain payoff. On-theme cube tools: spell-chaining, free / cost-reduced
spells, card-draw engines, coin-flip chance payoffs, copy effects, and untap bursts. No FIRE burn-storm
kill engines, no swingy bombs — everything here is decision-dense and self-limiting.

## Notes per pick

**Frenetic Efreet** (MIR 1996). The oldest, most on-theme card here: a 2/1 flyer with a free coin-flip
that either phases it out (dodging removal / sweepers, untaps free) or kills it. Pure Odyssey-era chance
energy, zero raw power. Best old-frame fit in the list.

**Odds // Ends** (DIS 2006). The Odds half ({U}{R}) flips a coin to either counter a target instant/sorcery
or copy it for you. A coin-flip payoff that is also a copy/counter spell — sits at the intersection of two
of the archetype's wanted roles, and is low-impact either way.

**Gelectrode** (GPT 2006). A 0/1 that untaps whenever you cast an instant or sorcery, letting it ping for 1
each time. It is the chain *engine* — the more spells you string together, the more value — without ever
being a fast kill. Excellent decision-density and storm-lite glue.

**Izzet Guildmage** (GPT 2006). For {2}{U} (instant) or {2}{R} (sorcery) copy a cheap spell you control.
A repeatable copy outlet that rewards casting multiple small spells in a chain. Mana-hungry, so honestly slow.

**Djinn Illuminatus** (GPT 2006). Seven-mana 3/3 flyer that gives all your instants/sorceries replicate.
A genuine storm-lite top-end, but every copy must be paid for, so it throttles itself and never combos out
for free. Fine as a singleton payoff in a very low-power list.

**Quicksilver Dagger** (APC 2001). Aura that gives a creature "{T}: 1 damage + draw a card." A slow,
removable card-advantage engine — exactly the draw-engine role wanted, with no explosive ceiling.

**Prophetic Bolt** (APC 2001). 4 damage plus dig four deep for a card. Mild card selection that keeps the
chain fed; on the higher-power edge of this list but still fair at five mana.

**Jhoira of the Ghitu** (FUT 2007). {2} + exile a nonland card to suspend it (4 counters). The deferred,
slowest-possible "cast for free" — pure setup and patience, no immediate advantage. Strong fit for a
build-around, slow archetype.

**Frenetic Sliver** (PLC 2007). Gives all Slivers a {0} coin-flip to blink-protect (or lose) themselves.
Quirky on-theme coin gamble; even off-tribe it can save itself. Pure fun, near-zero power.

**Nin, the Pain Artist** (RTR 2012 ⚑ post-2004). {X}{U}{R}, {T}: deal X to a creature, its controller draws
X. A symmetric draw engine / political removal — can fuel your own draw-your-deck plan by pointing it at
your own creature. Flag: 2012 set, newer frame.

**Goblin Electromancer** (RTR 2012 ⚑ post-2004). Instant and sorcery spells cost {1} less — the canonical
storm-lite enabler. Cheap, fragile, decision-relevant. Flag: 2012, post-2004; include only if a newer frame
is acceptable.

## Borderline / maybeboard

- **Invoke the Firemind** ({X}{U}{U}{R}, GPT 2006) — modal "draw X" or "X damage"; clean but the draw mode can
  get swingy at high X.
- **Mercurial Chemister** ({3}{U}{R}, RTR 2012) — {U},{T}: draw two; solid card engine, but a repeatable
  draw-two-per-turn may be slightly above the curve and is post-2004.
- **Cunning Wish** ({2}{U}, mono-U — not gold, noted for the pilot) — toolbox tutor; out of scope (single colour).
- **Inside Out** ({1}{U/R}, EVE 2008) — P/T swap + draw a card; cantrip trick, marginal.
- **Wee Dragonauts** ({1}{U}{R}, GPT 2006) — prowess-style beater; aggro flavour, off the lab-storm plan.
- **Frenetic Sliver / Frenetic Efreet** pair well if you want a small coin-flip sub-theme.
- **Stitch in Time** — coin-flip extra turn — is a great fit but is ALREADY in the cube (see below).
