# {B}{R} Rakdos — Hellbent Madness

A grindy aggro-disruption deck rewarded for an *empty hand* (hellbent) and for *discarding / casting from the bin* (madness). Empty your hand fast, then turn that emptiness into pressure and inevitability. Decision-dense: every card asks "do I dump my hand now or hold the trigger?"

The mechanic's spiritual home is **Dissension (DIS, 2006)** — the only set with the Hellbent keyword — and **Odyssey block (2001-03)** for madness/discard glue. Those are the prime-era pulls; a couple of strong reprint-era fits are flagged below.

## Notes per pick

**Dread Slag (DIS)** — The signature hellbent creature: an 8/8 that gets -4/-4 *per card in hand*, so it's a 0/0 with a full grip and an 8/8 trample-less beater only once you're empty. Exactly the build-around the archetype wants and laughably bad if you misbuild around it. Top of the list.

**Anthem of Rakdos (DIS)** — Attack-trigger anthem that pings you for 1 per attacker, plus a hellbent clause doubling all your damage. The self-damage and the "only when empty" gate keep it from being a FIRE bomb; it's a slow inevitability piece that punishes you if you stumble.

**Gobhobbler Rats (DIS)** — Clean two-drop hellbent payoff: a 2/2 that becomes a 3/2 with regeneration once your hand is empty. Unremarkable on its own (which is the point) and a curve-filler that rewards the plan.

**Jagged Poppet (DIS)** — A 3/4 that discards cards equal to damage taken (helping you go hellbent), and forces the defending player to discard when you connect empty-handed. Fiddly, double-edged, never swingy — ideal decision-density.

**Twinstrike (DIS)** — Modal-ish removal: 2 damage to each of two creatures normally, or *destroy both* if hellbent. Five mana is steep, the reward is conditional, so it stays low-power while paying off the empty hand.

**Avatar of Discord (DIS)** — A 5/3 flyer for three hybrid mana that you must sacrifice unless you discard two cards on ETB. That cost simultaneously empties your hand toward hellbent and stocks the graveyard for madness/recursion. Dies to a stiff breeze; a glass-cannon enabler, not a bomb.

**Lim-Dûl's Paladin (ALL, 1996)** — The oldest fit and a genuine old-frame curio: each upkeep you may discard a card or it sacrifices itself and draws you one — a repeatable hand-emptying outlet attached to a body with bizarre blocked/unblocked combat text. Pre-modern aesthetic, low power, high decisions.

**Rakdos Guildmage (DIS)** — A 2/2 with a discard-fueled -2/-2 removal mode (and a separate token mode). The discard activation is a slow but real hellbent enabler that converts dead cards into spot removal; mana cost keeps it honest.

**Bloodhall Priest (EMN, 2016) — FLAG post-2004.** The cleanest single-card statement of the archetype: a 4/5 that deals 2 to any target on ETB/attack *while hellbent*, and carries Madness {1}{B}{R}. If you allow a post-block fit, this is the best one — but note it's mid-power and a touch above the floor, so consider it borderline.

**Terminal Agony (MH2, 2021) — FLAG post-2004.** Vanilla "destroy target creature" sorcery, upgraded by Madness {B}{R}: pitch it to a discard outlet, cast it for cheap. The madness mode is the whole reason it's here. Modern set, but the printed effect is plain removal — power-appropriate.

## Borderline / maybeboard

- **Hellhole Rats (DIS)** — ETB discard + face damage equal to the pitched card's MV; a discard-themed beater but no hellbent/madness payoff.
- **Pain Magnification (DIS)** — "deal 3+ to an opponent → they discard"; a discard *engine pointed at the opponent*, off-axis from your own empty-hand plan.
- **Rakdos Augermage (DIS)** — symmetric reveal-and-discard; helps churn toward hellbent but it's clunky and partly helps the opponent.
- **Chainer, Nightmare Adept (C19)** — discard-to-cast-creatures-from-graveyard engine; great theme fit but a Commander-set legend, post-2004, edges toward value-engine power.
- **Olivia, Mobilized for War / Anje Falkenrath** — discard-matters madness lords, both post-2004 and a bit too efficient/snowbally for the floor.
- **Avatar of Discord** also reads as borderline if you find 5/3-flying-for-3 too pushy; included above as a top pick on the strength of its enabler text.
