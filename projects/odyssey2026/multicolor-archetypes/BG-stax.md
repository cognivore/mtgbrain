# {B}{G} Golgari — Stax

Grindy resource-denial / sacrifice deck that treats the graveyard as a resource and recurs lands and permanents. Wants symmetric attrition, sac engines, and recursion — not bombs.

## Per-pick notes

- **Savra, Queen of the Golgari** — the archetypal Golgari sac engine. Sacrificing a black creature edicts every other player; sacrificing a green creature gains life. Pair with Golgari Germination tokens for a symmetric grind that wears opponents' boards down. The defining build-around for this slot.
- **Dark Heart of the Wood** — old-frame (The Dark, 1994) land-sacrifice lifegain. Slots cleanly into the lands-as-resource plan: sac a Forest you've already cracked via Squandered Resources, buy time, and recur it with Crucible of Worlds. Pure attrition, no swing.
- **Golgari Rotwurm** — a 5/4 with a free repeatable sac outlet that drains 1. Turns dying tokens and chump blockers into reach and inevitability. Honest body, slow clock.
- **Golgari Germination** — every nontoken creature death makes a Saproling, so removal and sac both refill the board. The fodder source that powers Savra, Rotwurm, and Smokestack (already in cube). Does nothing alone — good low-power glue.
- **Woodwraith Strangler** — a 2/2 that exiles creatures from your graveyard to regenerate. A sticky blocker that grinds combat while slowly emptying the yard; quietly anti-synergistic with dredge, so it's a deliberate role-player, not auto-include.
- **Grave-Shell Scarab** — sac-to-draw on a 4/4 plus Dredge 1, so it keeps coming back from the graveyard for repeatable card advantage. Slow, grindy value exactly on-theme.
- **Gleancrawler** — at end of turn returns to hand every creature that died from the battlefield that turn. A recursion backbone for a sacrifice deck; the 6/6 trample body is sizable but the value is the slow loop, not a fast kill.
- **Drooling Groodion** — a sac outlet that converts spare creatures into a +2/+2 / -2/-2 pump-and-shrink. Repeatable removal tied to fodder; mana-hungry enough to stay fair.
- **Golgari Guildmage** — a 2/2 with sac-to-return-a-creature and a +1/+1 counter mode. Toolbox recursion at low rate (heavy activation costs), supporting the counter sub-theme (Crucible/Nested Shambler).
- **Doomgape** — FLAG: Eventide, 2008 (post-2004, newer frame). Forces you to sacrifice a creature each upkeep for life. Symmetric self-tax that rewards a wide token board and punishes a thin one — a genuine stax-flavored cost, not a bomb. Include only if a 2008 card is acceptable.

## Borderline / maybeboard

- **Vulturous Zombie** {3}{B}{G} (RAV) — grows off opponents' graveyards; fun, but a snowballing flyer leans more midrange-beater than grind. Maybeboard.
- **Mazirek, Kraul Death Priest** {3}{B}{G} (C15, 2015) — anthem-on-every-sacrifice; too new and too strong (can end games off one mass-sac). Note, mark too_strong.
- **Bloodbond March** {2}{B}{G} (RAV) — wildly symmetric mass-recursion; chaotic and swingy rather than grindy. Maybeboard.
- **Cadaverous Bloom** {3}{B}{G} (MIR, 1996) — hand-as-mana ritual; combo-enabler, off-theme for stax. Note only.
- **Death Mutation** {6}{B}{G} (APC, 2001) — expensive removal + tokens; fine filler, low priority.
- **Spiritmonger** {3}{B}{G} (APC, 2001) — iconic old-frame 6/6, but a clean beater, not a stax piece. Too strong / off-role.
- **Grim Feast** {1}{B}{G} (MIR, 1996) — graveyard-triggered lifegain at the cost of self-damage; cute attrition tech, maybeboard.

## Already in cube

The following BG-stax pieces are already on the cube list and are NOT counted as new finds:
- **Smokestack** (B/G colour-shift) — the symmetric soot-counter sacrifice engine; the locked Golgari anchor.
- **Squandered Resources** (VIS, 1997) — land-sacrifice burst-mana sink.
- **Pernicious Deed** (APC, 2001) — symmetric sac-reset (already flagged a high-end blowout, but present).

Existing mono-colour payoffs the gold cards support: Terravore, Mortivore, Crucible of Worlds, Nested Shambler.
