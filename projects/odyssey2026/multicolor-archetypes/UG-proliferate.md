# {U}{G} Simic — Proliferate / Counters

Gold cards that *make* counters (charge / +1/+1 / fade / age / growth), *multiply* them, or *pay them off* slowly — feeding the cube's existing Saproling Burst / Tangle Wire / Dreamtide Whale / Simic Ascendancy counter shell. Low-power, decision-dense, no FIRE bombs.

## Era reality check (read first)

There is essentially **no pre-2004 old-frame {U}{G} gold pool** for this theme. {U}{G} gold cards did not exist until Invasion/Apocalypse (2001), and the only Mirage-era "blue-green-ish" counter cards (Malignant Growth, Warping Wurm) are barely on-theme. Simic as a *counters* guild is a 2005-onward (Ravnica) invention, and proliferate is a 2010 (Scars of Mirrodin) keyword. So the honest answer: the best fits are **2005-2019 Ravnica/Gatecrash/Return-to-Ravnica/Eldraine-era commons & uncommons** in old-ish frame or modern frame. All non-pre-2004 picks below are flagged with set + year. Older candidates (Mirage 1996, Apocalypse 2001) are listed but are weak.

## Notes per pick

**Simic Guildmage** (DIS 2006) — `{1}{G}: Move a +1/+1 counter from target creature onto another with the same controller.` plus an aura-mover mode. A clean, cheap counter-mover with old-Ravnica frame. Does nothing flashy alone; rewards a board that already has counters (Saproling Burst tokens don't get counters, but graft/evolve creatures do). Pure decision-density.

**Zameck Guildmage** (GTC 2013) — `{G}{U}: each creature you control enters with an extra +1/+1 counter` and `{G}{U}, remove a +1/+1 counter: draw a card.` Two abilities that both make and cash in counters — a textbook Simic counters engine at the lowest power band. Mana-hungry, so never oppressive.

**Combine Guildmage** (RNA 2019) — the spiritual reprint of the above pair: tap to give all your creatures an extra counter this turn, or tap to move a counter. Slower (taps itself) so even gentler than Zameck.

**Plaxcaster Frogling** (DIS 2006) — Graft 3 means it *arrives carrying counters it hands out* to every creature that enters, and `{2}: shroud` protects a counter-creature. Old-frame, on-curve, and a great glue card for the archetype.

**Fathom Mage** (GTC 2013) — Evolve plus "whenever a +1/+1 counter is put on this, you may draw." The marquee *payoff*: any counter source (graft, proliferate, Zameck) turns into raw cards. A 1/1 body keeps it honest and removable.

**Bred for the Hunt** (DGM 2013) — `Whenever a creature you control with a +1/+1 counter on it deals combat damage to a player, draw a card.` A cheap enchantment payoff that asks you to actually connect with counter-creatures. No board impact by itself, so very fair.

**Lorescale Coatl** (ARB 2009) — `Whenever you draw a card, put a +1/+1 counter on it.` Self-contained slow-grow threat that loves any blue card-draw; pairs with Fathom Mage for a gentle engine. 2/2 for 3 with no evasion = beatable.

**Gilder Bairn** (EVE 2008) — `{2}{G/U}, {Q}: Double each kind of counter on target permanent.` The cube's best generic counter-*doubler* at low rarity: doubles +1/+1, **charge**, **fade**, **age**, slumber, growth (works with Simic Ascendancy, Tangle Wire's fade, Dreamtide Whale). Needs an untap, so it's a build-around, not a bomb.

**Winterthorn Blessing** (MID 2021, flag late) — put a +1/+1 counter on yours, tap-down theirs, with **flashback**. Two counter placements over a game for one card; modern frame but firmly low-power.

**Growth Curve** (Strixhaven side set 2021, flag late) — `Put a +1/+1 counter on target creature you control, then double the +1/+1 counters on it.` One-mana counter accelerant; trivial alone, satisfying on an evolve/graft target. Cheap and fair.

**Sturdy Hatchling** (EVE 2008) — enters with four **-1/-1 counters**; each green or blue spell removes one. A counter-*manipulation* puzzle in old frame: you grow it by playing your colors, and -1/-1 counters give the archetype another counter type to interact with. Low power, all decisions.

**Malignant Growth** (MIR 1996) — the only genuine old-frame {U}{G} counter card. Cumulative upkeep (age counters) plus growth counters that feed *opponents* extra cards and damage. Symmetric, janky, slow — included strictly for the pre-2004 aesthetic; weakest pick and arguably a maybeboard.

## Borderline / maybeboard

- **Vorel of the Hull Clade** (DGM 2013) — `{G}{U}, {T}: double each kind of counter on target artifact/creature/land.` Stronger doubler than Gilder Bairn; flagged borderline only because it can snowball a graft/evolve creature fast. Still fair at low power.
- **Shambleshark** (GTC 2013) — flash + evolve 2/1; a clean cheap counter-creature with surprise value. Filler-grade enabler.
- **Merfolk Skydiver** (WAR 2019) — flyer that drops a counter on ETB and has an *actual proliferate* activation. The cleanest "proliferate" body at common-ish power; flag WAR (2019).
- **Quandrix Pledgemage** (STX 2021) — magecraft grows itself on instants/sorceries; gentle spells-matter counter creature.
- **Shapers of Nature** (XLN 2017) — `{3}{G}: counter on a creature` / `{2}{U}, remove a counter: draw.` A clunky one-card Zameck engine; mana-intensive enough to be safe.
- **Hadana's Climb** (RIX 2018) — slow combat-trigger counter enchantment that flips into a payoff land; build-around, low ceiling, but flip side ("double a creature's power") edges toward swingy — vet carefully.
- **Aeromunculus / Sharktocrab / Zegana, Utopian Speaker** (RNA 2019) — adapt-based counter creatures; fine filler, slightly above floor.
- **Pygmy Hippo / Gaea's Skyfolk / Wood Sage** (VIS/APC/TMP) — old-frame {U}{G} bodies with **no** counter relevance; listed only to confirm the pre-2004 well is dry.
