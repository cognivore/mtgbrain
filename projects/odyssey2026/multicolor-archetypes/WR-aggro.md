# {W}{R} Boros — Aggro

A go-wide / token / combat-focused deck whose payoff is "opponent at 0 life." It wins by deploying cheap, awkward-to-block creatures, propping them up with small anthems and combat tricks, and grinding the last points with reach. Low power throughout — no FIRE-tier two-drops, no overstatted bombs. The deck has to *work* for the kill.

## Era note (important)

True pre-2004 WR **gold** is almost nonexistent. Invasion block printed essentially no aggressive WR creatures; Apocalypse gave us only **Goblin Legionnaire** and **Flowstone Charger**. The entire "Boros aggro" creature identity was *invented* in Ravnica (2005) with the Boros guild. So for this archetype the honest pre-2004 pool is tiny, and the best fits are 2005-06 Ravnica-block gold creatures. Per the cube's "post-2004 creatures only rarely" rule, every creature pick below is flagged with its era, and I have leaned on **non-creature** picks (anthems, token makers, tricks) where I can to stay pre-/early-frame and respect that rule.

## Per-pick notes

- **Goblin Legionnaire** — The cleanest pre-2004 fit and the headline pick for "historic." A {R}{W} 2/2 that, when it has outlived its blocking job, sacs for 2 damage to the face (reach to close) or 2 prevention to save an attacker. Pure Boros: aggressive early, flexible late, never a bomb.
- **Assemble the Legion** — A non-creature go-wide engine that snowballs hasty 1/1 tokens each upkeep. It is a genuine payoff for the deck. Caveat: the existing Multicolor worksheet already flags it `too_strong` for this cube's power band (it dominates if left alone). Include only if you want one real "build-around" token engine; otherwise treat as borderline.
- **Flame-Kin Zealot** — The quintessential go-wide finisher: ETB gives your whole board +1/+1 and haste, turning a developed board into a surprise lethal swing. The body itself is a plain 2/2, so it stays low power. 2005 creature — flag.
- **Skyknight Legionnaire** — The model "fair" Boros gold creature: 2/2 flying, haste for {1}{R}{W}. Evasive and immediately aggressive without being oversized. 2005 creature — flag.
- **Boros Swiftblade** — 1/2 double strike for {R}{W}. On its own it is small and dies to a stiff breeze, which is exactly the low-power, decision-dense profile wanted: it is only good if you invest a pump or anthem, rewarding the deck's combat-trick play. 2005 creature — flag.
- **Boros Recruit** — A {R/W} 1/1 first strike one-drop, castable off a single land of either color. Trivial stats, good curve-filler and trick target. 2005 creature — flag.
- **Akroan Hoplite** — A 1/2 that attacks as a +X/+0 where X is your attacker count — a true go-wide payoff that is harmless when you are behind. 2013 creature — flag (use sparingly).
- **Rally the Righteous** — Non-creature alpha-strike: Radiance untaps a creature and every creature sharing its color (so all your attackers) and gives them +2/+0. A blowout combat trick that fits the "swing for the kill" plan without adding a body. Great glue card for the archetype.
- **Martial Glory** — A cheap, modest {R}{W} trick: +3/+0 to one creature and +0/+3 to another, splitting offense and defense in one combat. Low ceiling, fair.
- **Weapons Trainer** — A team +1/+0 anthem stapled to a 3/2 body, conditional on controlling an Equipment. If the cube runs even a few cheap Equipment it is a respectable, non-bomb anthem creature. 2016 creature — flag (use sparingly).

## Borderline / maybeboard

- **Boros Guildmage** ({R/W}{R/W}, RAV 2005) — repeatable haste / first-strike granting; utility 2/2, slightly more grindy than aggressive.
- **Sunhome Enforcer** ({2}{R}{W}, RAV 2005) — 2/4 lifelink-on-damage + firebreathing; more of a midrange wall than an aggressor, but a fine grindy beater.
- **Thundersong Trumpeter** ({R}{W}, RAV 2005) — taps down a blocker each turn to push damage; tempo enabler, low impact.
- **Iroas's Champion** ({1}{R}{W}, ORI 2015) — 2/2 double strike, evasion-light but punchy; clean but post-2004.
- **Flowstone Charger** ({2}{R}{W}, APC 2001) — the *other* genuinely pre-2004 creature; +3/-3 on attack makes it a 5/2 swinger, but the self-shrink is awkward. Honorable historic mention.
- **Agrus Kos, Wojek Veteran** ({3}{R}{W}, RAV 2005) — attack-trigger anthem on a 3/3 legend; borderline on power for a 4-drop, fine as a top-end.
- **Searing Meditation** ({1}{R}{W}, RAV 2005) — only relevant if the cube has a lifegain subtheme; not core Boros aggro.
- **Outlaws' Merriment** ({1}{R}{W}{W}, ELD 2019) — excellent token engine but a very modern card; skip unless you want a newer build-around.

## Already in cube

Neither is duplicated above. The worksheet also notes **Assemble the Legion** and **Powerstone Minefield** as previously-considered WR cards (Assemble flagged `too_strong`), so weigh Assemble accordingly.
