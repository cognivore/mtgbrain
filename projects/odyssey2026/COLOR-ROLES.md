# oddysey2026 — Colour Roles (the pie at low power)

What each colour *is* in this cube. The creatures are all from the curated `/odysseyblock` pool
(weak, stall-breaking, enabling); the non-creature tools come from any era. Counts are the cube's
worksheet sizes.

---

## White — the wall, the enchantment, the tax (47)
White does not race; it **outlasts**. Its creatures are Birds, Clerics, and Nomads — cheap fliers
and **threshold** bodies that wall the ground then fly over it (Mystic Zealot, Commander Eesha,
Battlewise Aven), prevention Clerics (Master Apothecary, Militant Monk), and the **Phantom
damage-shields** (Phantom Nomad/Flock) that turn +1/+1 counters into renewable blockers.
**Tools:** the cube's entire **enchantment / enchantress** suite (Replenish, Humility, Opalescence
partner, Parallax Wave *fading*, Sphere of Safety, Mesa Enchantress, Sterling Grove), the
**pillowfort/tax** layer (Ghostly Prison, Story Circle, Solitary Confinement, Cage of Hands), and the
**life-total alt-wins** (Test of Endurance, Near-Death Experience, Transcendence).
**Role:** prison glue, stall walls, and lifegain — the colour that makes the long game possible.

## Blue — the engine, the combo, the tempo (50)
Blue is the **enabler**. Creatures are Cephalid self-millers (Looter, Vandal, Sage) that fuel
graveyard/threshold/deck-out, plus evasive wizards and tempo fliers.
**Tools:** the **High Tide** free-spell package (Snap, Frantic Search, Reset, Turnabout), **Islands
prison** (Spreading Seas, Back to Basics), **control** (Standstill, Day's Undoing, Rewind), **mill /
deck-out wins** (Brain Freeze, Sphinx's Tutelage, Jace Wielder, Mana Severance, Narcomoeba), and a
share of **proliferate** (Steady Progress, Tezzeret's Gambit).
**Role:** the combo and card-flow engine — and the tempo that protects a fragile win.

## Black — the deepest colour: fuel, disruption, kills (55)
Black is the **most-loaded** colour, the meeting point of half the cube's themes. Creatures are
Zombies, threshold horrors, madness bodies, and sacrifice fodder/outlets.
**Tools:** **rituals** (Dark/Cabal/Songs — mana *and* storm count), **discard / hellbent** (Raven's
Crime, Collective Brutality, Nihilistic Glee, Shrieking Affliction), **tutors** (Infernal Tutor,
Demonic Consultation, Tainted Pact, Doomsday), the **storm payoff** (Tendrils of Agony), the
**Ad Nauseam** centerpiece, **life-as-a-resource** (Greed, Bond of Agony, Font of Agonies, Death's
Shadow), and the colour-shifted **hand-punishers** (Black Vise, The Rack, Ebony Owl Netsuke).
**Role:** fuel, disruption, and the most combo kills — the spine of BR storm, UB combo, BG sac, WB life.

## Red — variance, the mana sink, aggression (45)
Red brings the cube's two signatures: **coin-flips** and the **big-red mana sink**. Creatures are
dwarves, barbarians, madness one-drops, and threshold/firebreathing beaters that break stalls
(Grim Lavamancer, Savage Firecat, Kamahl).
**Tools:** the **coin-flip lane** (Chance Encounter win, Goblin Bomb, Game of Chaos, Fiery Gambit,
Molten Birth — all feeding Krark's Thumb), the **mana sink / cumulative-upkeep** package (Braids of
Fire, Magmatic Core, Heart of Bogardan, Comet Storm), **storm fuel/payoff** (Pyretic/Seething rituals,
Grapeshot, Empty the Warrens, Lightning Storm), **wheels** (Wheel of Fortune, Reforge the Soul), and
**rummage/madness** (Faithless Looting, Fiery Temper, Violent Eruption).
**Role:** randomness, escalating mana, aggression, and the storm engine.

## Green — the board, the ramp, the counters (43)
Green is the **bodies and the mana**. Creatures are madness beaters (Wild Mongrel, Basking Rootwalla,
Arrogant Wurm), Squirrel/Saproling token-makers, mana dorks (Werebear, Diligent Farmhand), threshold
stall-breakers, and the **counter-movers** (Forgotten Ancient, Quarry Hauler, Chlorophant).
**Tools:** **ramp** (Wild Growth, Rampant Growth, Carpet of Flowers), **token engines** (Squirrel Nest,
flashback tokens) that feed Opposition and Smokestack, **fading** (Saproling Burst), and the
**Helix Pinnacle** alt-win mana sink.
**Role:** the widest board, the ramp for big-red and High Tide, and the fuel for Opposition + the
counters engine.

## Colorless — the shared toolbox (47)
The artifact backbone every deck reaches into: **stax/prison** (Winter Orb, Static Orb, Storage
Matrix, the Spheres, Crawlspace), **counter alt-wins + proliferate** (Darksteel Reactor, Contagion
Engine, Throne of Geth, Spawning Pit), the **wheel-lock** (Teferi's Puzzle Box, Anvil of Bogardan,
Howling Mine, Font of Mythos, Mind's Eye), **lantern mill** (Codex Shredder, Lantern of Insight,
Mindcrank, Pyxis — errata'd to clock alone), **life-swap** (Mirror Universe, Soul Conduit),
**combo enablers** (Sundial of the Infinite, Leveler, Lion's Eye Diamond, Krark's Thumb),
**big-red sinks** (Trading Post, Worn Powerstone), and **low-power fixing rocks**.
**Role:** the connective hardware — it's where the locks and alternate wins that *any* colour pair
can run actually live.

## Lands — fixing that also builds around (41)
Lands do real work here, not just fixing. **Fixing:** allied + enemy **filter lands** (Skycloud
Expanse, Sunken Ruins, …) and the block **sac-taplands**. **Build-around utility:** **storage lands**
(Calciform Pools & co. — storage counters = proliferate targets), **threshold painlands** (Cephalid
Coliseum self-mill, Barbarian Ring, Cabal Pit), **Karn's Bastion** (proliferate) and **Nesting
Grounds** (move a counter), **Glacial Chasm** (cumulative-upkeep pillowfort), **wheel lands** (Geier
Reach, Mikokoro), **hellbent** (Sea Gate Wreckage), and the **ONS cycling lands** for graveyard/discard.
**Role:** low-power colour fixing *plus* a second layer of engine pieces hidden in the mana base.

### One-line summary
- **W** prison/walls/lifegain · **U** engine/combo/tempo · **B** fuel/disruption/kills ·
  **R** variance/mana-sink/aggression · **G** board/ramp/counters.
- **Colorless** = the shared lock-and-alt-win toolbox · **Lands** = fixing that doubles as engines.
